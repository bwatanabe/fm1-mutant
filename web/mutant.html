FM-1 MUTANT

An experimental multi-engine sound instrument built by combining existing open-source DSP engines. 27 models: 24 GATOR/Plaits, Felucca LOFI, JIANT VOICE, and CHIP VOX—the first hybrid, routing LOFI oscillator audio through JIANT's formant resonators.

SourceDevelopment

1 · Try the FM-1 in your browser

This is Joakim Eriksson's full FM-1 emulator, running actual firmware in WebAssembly. It has audio, screen, encoders, keyboard and MIDI input. It initially loads its own default firmware—not Mutant.

Open full emulatorDownload Mutant .fwsc

To audition Mutant, download the .fwsc above, open the full emulator, use Open .fwsc…, then press Start. To reach CHIP VOX (D3) with one-button emulator input: from HOME press HOME to cycle Plaits → LOFI → VOICE → CHIP VOX → Plaits. From any other screen, first press HOME to return to HOME. You can also send MIDI CC19=127 to select CHIP VOX directly, then play notes and vary the HOME K3 VOWEL control. The browser preview runs exactly the compiled firmware image, not a recreated synth engine. The emulated CPU load may differ from real hardware.
